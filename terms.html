2. Bookings, Payment & Security Bond

2.1 Deposit

A booking is only confirmed upon receipt of a non-refundable 50% deposit of the total invoice amount.

2.2 Final Payment

The remaining balance must be paid in full no later than 7 days prior to the event date.

2.3 Security Bond

A refundable Security Bond of $250 applies to all soft play and relevant equipment hire bookings.

2.4 Bond Refund & Deductions

The Security Bond will be inspected and processed following collection of the equipment. Audora Co reserves the right to deduct reasonable costs from the bond for:

  • Excessive soilage or staining requiring specialized cleaning;
  • Loss, theft, or physical damage to equipment; or
  • Failure to adhere to access, pack-down, or collection terms.

2.5 Recovery of Excess Costs

If damage, loss, or cleaning costs exceed $250, the customer agrees to pay the additional reasonable costs incurred by Audora Co within 7 days of written invoice demand.

3. Cancellations & Rescheduling

3.1 Customer Cancellation (> 14 Days)

If you cancel more than 14 days prior to the scheduled event date, all monies paid will be fully refunded.

3.2 Customer Cancellation (≤ 14 Days)

If you cancel within 14 days of the event date, amounts paid are non-refundable. Instead, the amount paid will be issued as a credit voucher valid for 6 months from the original event date, subject to equipment availability.

3.3 Cancellation by Audora Co

If Audora Co must cancel a booking due to circumstances within our reasonable control (e.g., equipment malfunction, illness), you will receive a 100% refund of all monies paid.

3.4 Weather Cancellations

Cancellations or modifications due to inclement weather are governed separately under Clause 6.

4. Site Preparation, Access & Customer Responsibilities

4.1 Active Adult Supervision

Soft play equipment is delivered and set up by Audora Co, but Audora Co does not remain at the event or provide supervision. The customer assumes full responsibility for ensuring that all equipment is actively supervised by a responsible adult (aged 18+) at all times.

4.2 Site Requirements

The customer must provide a clean, dry, level, and safe surface with clear, safe access. The customer must notify Audora Co prior to booking of any access impediments, including stairs, steep inclines, narrow pathways, long carrying distances (>20 meters from vehicle), or restricted parking. Additional access fees may apply if undisclosed access difficulties delay setup.

4.3 Rules of Play & Prohibited Items

To prevent injury and equipment damage, the customer must strictly enforce the following rules:

Strictly Prohibited

No food, drinks, shoes, face paint, glitter, party poppers, confetti, sharp objects, ink/pens, or pets are permitted on or near the equipment.

Capacity & Age Limits

Equipment is designed for young children (ages 0-5 unless otherwise specified). Adults may enter the area solely to assist a child.

4.4 Relocation

Equipment must not be moved, relocated, or dismantled from the position installed by Audora Co without prior written authorization.

5. Soft Play Equipment

5.1 Ownership

All soft play equipment remains the exclusive property of Audora Co at all times.

5.2 Care & Loss

The customer is responsible for the care and security of all equipment from setup until collection by Audora Co. The customer will be charged the reasonable repair or replacement cost for stolen, unreturned or damaged equipment.

6. Outdoor Setup & Wet Weather Policy

6.1 Strict Weather Limits

Soft play equipment cannot safely be used when wet or subjected to extreme environmental conditions. Audora Co reserves the absolute right to refuse, cancel, modify, or halt an outdoor setup if weather conditions are deemed unsafe or likely to damage equipment.

Unsafe conditions include:

  • Rainfall (active rain or >50% forecast probability during event hours);
  • Wet, muddy, or slippery ground surfaces;
  • Sustained winds exceeding 30 km/h or wind gusts exceeding 40 km/h; or
  • Ambient air temperatures reaching or exceeding 35°C.

6.2 Indoor Backup Requirement

For all outdoor bookings, the customer must have a suitable indoor or completely weather-protected backup location available.

6.3 Weather Cancellation & Rescheduling

If unsafe weather prevents an outdoor event from proceeding and the customer has no suitable indoor alternative:

  • Monies paid will not be refunded.
  • The customer will receive a credit valid for 6 months from the original date to reschedule, subject to availability.

6.4 Mid-Event Weather Changes

If unexpected rain, high winds, or extreme weather occurs after setup, the customer must immediately cease use of the equipment, move unattached items indoors if safe to do so, and cover/protect soft play mats and gear. No refunds or credits are issued for events interrupted by weather after setup has been completed.
