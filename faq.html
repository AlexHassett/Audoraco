Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hiring soft play with Audora.

Play & Hire

Where do you deliver?

Our standard delivery areas include the Sutherland Shire, St George, Bankstown, Liverpool and surrounding South West Sydney areas. We can also travel outside these areas, with an additional delivery fee calculated based on location.

How much is delivery and setup?

Standard soft play delivery and setup is $80. Additional charges may apply for locations outside our standard service area or venues with unusual access requirements. We'll confirm any additional cost before your booking is finalised.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking soft play at least 2 weeks in advance, subject to availability. Earlier bookings are encouraged where possible, particularly for weekends and popular dates.

How long is soft play hire?

Our standard soft play hire period is four hours.

Do you deliver and set up the soft play?

Yes. Audora delivers, professionally sets up and packs down your soft play so everything is ready for the little guests to enjoy.

Can soft play be used outdoors?

Yes, provided it is placed on a clean, dry, level and suitable surface. Equipment cannot be set up on dirt or sand, and all outdoor bookings remain subject to Audora's weather requirements.

What happens if it rains?

If unsafe weather means an outdoor booking cannot proceed and there is no suitable indoor or weather-protected alternative, the amount paid will become a credit valid for six months, subject to availability.

Do children need to be supervised?

Yes. Active supervision by an adult aged 18 or over is required at all times. Audora sets up and packs down the equipment but does not remain at the event to supervise.

What are the soft play rules?

Soft play is intended for children aged 0-5 unless otherwise specified. Please keep food, drinks, shoes, face paint, glitter, party poppers, confetti, sharp objects, pens, ink and pets away from the equipment.

Bookings & Payments

What deposit is required?

A 50% deposit secures your hire booking and event date.

When is final payment due?

The remaining balance is due seven days before your event.

Is there a security bond?

Yes. A refundable $250 security bond applies to soft play hire.

What is your cancellation policy?

For cancellations more than 14 days before your event, monies paid are refunded. For cancellations within 14 days, monies paid are non-refundable but become a credit valid for six months from the original event date, subject to availability.
