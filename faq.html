Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hiring soft play with Audora.

Play & Hire

Where in Sydney do you deliver soft play?

We provide soft play hire across Sydney, with standard delivery areas including the Sutherland Shire, St George, Bankstown, Liverpool and surrounding South West Sydney suburbs. We may also be able to travel beyond these areas; any additional delivery fee will be confirmed based on your event location before your booking is finalised.

How much is delivery and setup?

Soft play delivery and setup is available starting from $60, depending on event location. Additional charges may apply for locations outside our standard service area or venues with unusual access requirements. We'll confirm the cost before your booking is finalised.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking soft play at least 2 weeks in advance, subject to availability. Earlier bookings are encouraged where possible, particularly for weekends and popular dates.

How long is soft play hire?

Our standard soft play hire is 6 hours in total, including approximately 60 minutes for setup, 4 hours of playtime for your celebration, and 60 minutes for pack down. Our team takes care of all setup and pack down, so you can simply enjoy your event.

Additional playtime can be arranged for an extra fee, subject to availability.

Do you deliver and set up the soft play?

Yes. Audora delivers, professionally sets up and packs down your soft play so everything is ready for the little guests to enjoy.

Can soft play be used outdoors?

Yes, provided it is placed on a clean, dry, level and suitable surface. Equipment cannot be set up on dirt or sand, and all outdoor bookings remain subject to Audora's weather requirements.

What happens if it rains?

If unsafe weather means an outdoor booking cannot proceed and there is no suitable indoor alternative, the amount paid will become a credit valid for 12 months from the original scheduled event date, subject to availability.

Do children need to be supervised?

Yes. Active supervision by an adult aged 18 or over is required at all times. Audora sets up and packs down the equipment but does not remain at the event to supervise.

What are the soft play rules?

Our soft play is designed for children aged 0–5 years, unless otherwise specified. To keep our play spaces safe and clean, please follow these rules:

  • No shoes (socks preferred).
  • No pets.
  • No face paint or glitter.
  • No food or drink.
  • No sharp objects, pens or ink.
  • No climbing on walls or barriers.
  • No rough play or diving.
  • Adult supervision is required at all times.

Please also keep party poppers and confetti away from the soft play equipment.

Bookings & Payments

What deposit is required?

A 50% deposit secures your hire booking and event date.

When is final payment due?

The remaining balance is due seven days before your event.

Is there a security bond?

Yes. A refundable $250 security bond applies to soft play hire.

What is your cancellation policy?

For cancellations more than 14 days before your event, monies paid are refunded. For cancellations within 14 days, monies paid are non-refundable but become a credit valid for six months from the original event date, subject to availability.
